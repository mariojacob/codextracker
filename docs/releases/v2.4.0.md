# CodexTracker 2.4.0 — Plan your usage with manual reset dates

Know when your next reset will happen? Set a manual deadline for each quota module and align your daily budget and pace marker with it.

## Manual reset planning

- Add a reset date and time directly inside a module using **Set reset**. Dates use your local timezone.
- Choose the affected quota when a module has multiple limits, such as a session limit and a weekly limit or separate model pools.
- Switch between **Automatic** and **Manual reset** display. The manual view recalculates the pace marker, budget-day segments and daily allowance up to your chosen deadline.
- A purple marker identifies your manual reset on the quota bar. In automatic mode, it appears when the deadline falls within the provider's current reset window.
- Edit or remove a saved deadline whenever your plans change. Editing an upcoming deadline keeps the plan's starting point, so the budget stays consistent.
- Saved deadlines and display choices survive app restarts. Expired deadlines are labeled and the display returns to automatic until you update them.
- Controls and labels are available in English and German, including in the app's compact window layout.

The consumed percentage continues to come from the provider. Manual dates are planning aids: they do not trigger a provider reset or redeem a reset credit. Statistics continue to follow the provider's reported reset windows. Modules that only report API balances or expenses do not have quota reset controls.

## Upgrading from 2.3.0

Quit CodexTracker completely, including its tray process, then replace the previous executable with `CodexTracker-2.4.0.exe`. Your existing settings, module order, saved credentials and local history are preserved. Automatic display remains the default until you configure a manual reset.
